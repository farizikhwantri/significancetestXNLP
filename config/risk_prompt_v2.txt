(A) Annex III categories and decision rules:
- Biometrics (non-banned):
  - Remote biometric identification systems (exclude biometric verification that confirms a person is who they claim to be).
  - Biometric categorisation systems inferring sensitive or protected attributes or characteristics.
  - Emotion recognition systems.
- Critical infrastructure:
  - Safety components in the management and operation of critical digital infrastructure, road traffic, and the supply of water, gas, heating, and electricity.
- Education and vocational training:
  - Determining access/admission/assignment to institutions; evaluating learning outcomes (incl. steering learning); assessing appropriate level; monitoring/detecting prohibited behavior during tests.
- Employment, workers management, and access to self-employment:
  - Recruitment/selection (targeted job ads, application filtering, candidate evaluation); promotion/termination; task allocation based on traits/behavior; performance monitoring/evaluation.
- Access to and enjoyment of essential public and private services:
  - Public authority assessments of eligibility to benefits/services (allocation/reduction/revocation/recovery); evaluating creditworthiness (except detecting financial fraud); classifying emergency calls (dispatch prioritization, urgent triage); risk assessment and pricing in health/life insurance.
- Law enforcement:
  - Assessing risk of becoming a crime victim; polygraphs; evaluating evidence reliability; assessing risk of offending/re-offending (not solely based on profiling or past behavior); profiling during criminal detections/investigations/prosecutions.
- Migration, asylum, and border control management:
  - Polygraphs; assessments of irregular migration or health risks; examination of asylum/visa/residence applications and related complaints; detecting/recognising/identifying individuals (except verifying travel documents).
- Administration of justice and democratic processes:
  - Researching/interpreting facts and applying the law to concrete facts; alternative dispute resolution; influencing elections/referenda or voting behavior (exclude tools that only organize/optimize/structure campaigns and do not directly interact with people).
